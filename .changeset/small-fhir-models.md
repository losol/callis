Add ready-made immutable `r4` (FHIR 4.0.1) and `r5` (FHIR 5.0.0) ModelProviders generated from pinned official definitions. Providers include resources, datatypes, inherited elements, choices, recursive backbones and primitive-to-System mappings. The existing core ModelProvider interface is unchanged.
